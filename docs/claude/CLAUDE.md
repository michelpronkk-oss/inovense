# Auterim working rules

Auterim is an AI workforce platform for businesses. The product understands a company before recommending controlled AI workforces.

Core loop: Connect → Understand → Diagnose → Recommend → Deploy → Measure → Improve

Use auterim.com for marketing, app.auterim.com for customer software, and admin.auterim.com for internal administration. Do not introduce portal.auterim.com as an active surface. Use hello@auterim.com, support@auterim.com, and noreply@auterim.com for public communication.

Preserve legacy cookies, localStorage keys, HubSpot properties, Slack/Nango events, database plan values, Dodo product IDs, and historical records. Do not change secrets or provider configuration without explicit approval.
